# Rentable houseboats near Lisbon (task 0.2a)

*Survey date: 2026-07-29.*

## The strategic winner

**[The Homeboat Company](https://vousair.pt/the-homeboat-company-os-barcos-casa-que-convidam-a-dormir-sobre-o-tejo/)** — actual mini floating houses moored at **Marina Parque das Nações**. Equipped like the real thing: AC + heating, induction hob, fridge, queen bed, wifi, terrace. Bookable via [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/43735417), [Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/pt/the-homeboat-company-parque-das-nacoes-lisboa-lisboa.html) or [Odisseias](https://www.odisseias.com/hoteis-e-viagens/experiencia/the-homeboat-company-lisboa-estadia-flutuante-em-barco-casa/173558).

Why this one first: it's not just a bed — it's **[DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md) option B's marina, experienced from the inside**. A 2–3 night stay yields:

- First real data for [SCN-002](../_scenarios/scn-002-a-week-of-january-gales.md) (comfort/noise/motion at berth), [SCN-003](../_scenarios/scn-003-back-to-back-video-calls.md) (work a real workday aboard — take the laptop), [SCN-004](../_scenarios/scn-004-a-normal-tuesday-for-marley.md) (check pet policy; if Marley can come, even better)
- Walk the pontoons, see who actually lives there, strike up the first informal liveaboard conversations (Phase 1.1 warm-up)
- Assess the marina itself: shelter, facilities, noise, surroundings at night — before SPK-1 is ever sent
