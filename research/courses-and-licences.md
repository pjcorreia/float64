# Courses and licences (task 0.1a)

*Survey date: 2026-07-29. Prices are advertised, unconfirmed.*

## The two candidate licences

| | Carta de Marinheiro | Carta de Patrão Local |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | **Daytime only**, ≤3 nm from coast, ≤10 nm from a porto de abrigo | **Day and night**, ≤6 nm from coast, ≤25 nm from a porto de abrigo |
| Boat size | **≤12 m** (adults), power per certification | **No size or power limit** |
| Prerequisite | none | none — can be taken directly |
| Typical course price | €89 (promo) – €450 | ~€500 |

## The decisive detail

The Marinheiro 12 m limit sits exactly on the Apartboat XL (12.00 m) — zero margin, and the XXL is out. The Overblue 44 (13.5 m) is **over the limit entirely**. So [DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md)'s option A (CE-B/C platform) *requires* Patrão Local anyway, and option B sits on the boundary. Night navigation also erases the daytime-return constraint that [SCN-001](../_scenarios/scn-001-weekend-crossing-from-oeiras.md) flagged.

**Working recommendation: skip Marinheiro, go straight to Patrão Local.** ~€130 more than a full-price Marinheiro course, and it future-proofs both DEC-004 branches.
