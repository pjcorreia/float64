**Given** a normal remote workday — six hours of calls and focus work,
**when** it happens aboard on a day with weather,
**then** connectivity, power, ergonomics and background noise are all office-grade.

## To answer

- Internet: 4G/5G coverage per marina, Starlink as backup, failover setup.
- Power budget of a full workday (laptop, monitor, router) vs battery/shore power.
- A real desk: where does the monitor live, and does it survive the boat moving?
- Noise on calls: halyards, wind, gulls, neighbours.
- Video-call background test: does it look professional or like a holiday?
