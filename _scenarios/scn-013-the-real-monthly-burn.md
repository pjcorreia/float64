**Given** the sales pitch says living afloat is cheap,
**when** every real cost is on one spreadsheet — berth, insurance, maintenance reserve, haul-out amortisation, fuel, utilities, depreciation,
**then** the monthly total compares honestly against the land apartment, and the purchase doesn't depend on optimism.
