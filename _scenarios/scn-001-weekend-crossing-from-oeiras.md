**Given** a La Mare Apartboat XL (CE-D, 12×5 m, ~0.5 m draft, sail-walls, 2 small outboards, ~5 kn) berthed at the Porto de Recreio de Oeiras,
**when** I try to cross the estuary on an ordinary weekend and anchor off the opposite bank,
**then** I should get there and back safely, legally and insured.

## Result: FAIL

The boat fits the marina (limits: 25 m × 6 m × 3 m draft), but the crossing runs through the throat of the estuary: main shipping channel, 2–3+ kn spring-tide currents, Atlantic swell through the bar, and the *nortada* filling in before lunch. A CE-D certification covers waves to 0.3 m — that exists a handful of glassy dawns per month in summer. Insurance outside design category: swampy. Full analysis: [research note](../research/oeiras-estuary-crossing.md).

Technically possible in rare hand-picked windows; imprudent in all others. As a *routine weekend* scenario, that's a fail.

## Generated requirements

- Oversized anchor for houseboat windage
- Anchor light
- Tide planning before every departure (the Alcochete/Montijo flats dry)
- Daytime-only returns (Marinheiro licence = daylight navigation)
- Dinghy — Marley needs shore leave, and nobody rows a house
