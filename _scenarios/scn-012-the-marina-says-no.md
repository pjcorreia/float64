title: The marina doesn't renew the contract

**Given** my home occupies a berth I don't own, under an annual contract,
**when** the marina declines to renew — policy change, redevelopment, new management, or liveaboards falling out of favour,
**then** I have somewhere to go that isn't a crisis.

## To answer

- Do Lisbon-area marinas officially allow liveaboards at all, or tolerate them? (This is SPK-1 — the single project-killing question.)
- Contract terms: notice periods, liveaboard clauses, price escalation history.
- Plan B berths: how many alternatives exist within commuting distance? Waiting lists?
- Mooring outside marinas: is a private mooring/anchorage legally possible on the Tejo (APL/DGRM)?
- This scenario defines the *portability* requirement: the home must be able to move to a plan-B berth under its own power — links [DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md).
