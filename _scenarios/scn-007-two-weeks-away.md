**Given** a normal trip — work travel or holidays,
**when** the boat sits unattended at the berth for two weeks,
**then** it's still exactly as I left it: dry, charged, moored and uninvaded.

## To answer

- Security: theft/boarding risk per marina, alarms, cameras.
- Marley logistics: kennel/sitter that works from a marina address.
- Insurance conditions for unattended periods.
