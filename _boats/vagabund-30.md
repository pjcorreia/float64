**float64 read:** the shape most directly comparable to the La Mare Apartboat XL for full-time living — similar footprint class, but €310k all-in vs the La Mare's ~€180k gross. What the extra buys: aluminium construction, serious insulation (80 mm PUR sandwich walls), thrusters included, 13.5 m length. Note: 13.5 m needs Patrão Local ([courses](../research/courses-and-licences.md)), and the EU-warranty surcharge is a real +€31k on the sticker if wanted in Portugal. Still CE-D → [DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md) option B only.
