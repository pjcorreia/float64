The reference houseboat: apartment-grade living space on a floating hull, walls of glass, real rooms.

**Verdict so far:** *conditional* — it passes as a home, but [SCN-001](../_scenarios/scn-001-weekend-crossing-from-oeiras.md) showed it fails the Oeiras-based weekend-crossing scenario (CE-D, 0.3 m wave limit, sail-walls, 5 kn). Viable only if the base is Parque das Nações and trips stay inside the Mar da Palha — i.e., it hangs on [DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md).

**Still to check:**

- Multihull/wide-beam surcharge at Lisbon marinas.
- Insurance terms for liveaboard use on the Tejo.
