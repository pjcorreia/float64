The reference houseboat: apartment-grade living space on a floating hull, walls of glass, real rooms.

**Verdict so far:** *conditional* — it passes as a home, but [SCN-001](../_scenarios/scn-001-weekend-crossing-from-oeiras.md) showed it fails the Oeiras-based weekend-crossing scenario (CE-D, 0.3 m wave limit, sail-walls, 5 kn). Viable only if the base is Parque das Nações and trips stay inside the Mar da Palha — i.e., it hangs on [DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md).

**Market data (2026-07-29):** new from [~€146k + VAT](https://mboat.eu/apart-xl/); a [2022 used example listed at €219k](https://www.boats.com/power-boats/2022-la-mare-houseboat-apartboat-xl-10079697/) in the Netherlands (equipped). Range anchors: an 11×4.2 m La Mare at €119.9k, a 15×5 m at €231k ([botentekoop](https://www.botentekoop.com/boats/make-la-mare/model-apartboat-houseboat/)). Licence note: 12.00 m sits *exactly* on the Carta de Marinheiro size limit — see [courses research](../research/courses-and-licences.md).

**Still to check:**

- Multihull/wide-beam surcharge at Lisbon marinas.
- Insurance terms for liveaboard use on the Tejo.
- Heating/insulation spec for a Portuguese winter (humidity is the enemy, per the telemetry plan).
