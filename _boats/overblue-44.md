**OUT OF THE RUNNING (2026-07-29): too expensive.** At ~2–3× the price of the houseboat alternatives (used 2017 listed ~US$429k vs Apartboat XL from ~€146k), it fails [SCN-013](../_scenarios/scn-013-the-real-monthly-burn.md) before that scenario is even run. Kept in the archive because the *reason* matters: it was the champion of [DEC-004](../plans/decisions.md)'s option A (seaworthy platform for an Oeiras base), and its rejection means **option A currently has no affordable candidate** — if Oeiras is to stay in play, the archive needs a used trawler or motorsailer in the €100–150k class instead.

Was the main candidate for the *other* branch of DEC-004: if Oeiras (and the estuary mouth) is a Must, the platform needs real seaworthiness without giving up apartment-grade living space. Power cats in this class aim at exactly that trade — at a price.

**Market data (2026-07-29):** the seaworthiness box is emphatically ticked — **CE category B standard** (Force 8+, 4 m waves), far beyond the CE-C the estuary mouth demands ([MBY review](https://www.mby.com/reviews/flybridges/overblue-44-review), [TradeABoat](https://www.tradeaboat.com.au/news-reviews/6705-overblue-44-houseboat)). 13.5 m × 4.2 m, twin 75 hp, 16 kn top, ~1000 nm range on 400 l. The 4.2 m beam is *narrower* than the Apartboat XL's 5 m — an easier berth conversation. But: 1.3 m draft (vs 0.5), and a [used 2017 listed at ~US$429k](https://www.yachtworld.com/yacht/2017-overblue-44-powercat-8256247/) — roughly **2–3× the La Mare**. At 13.5 m it also requires Patrão Local, not Marinheiro ([courses research](../research/courses-and-licences.md)).

**Reinstatement condition:** a European used example at houseboat money (≤ ~€180k) would reopen the file — the CE-B certification and 4.2 m beam remain genuinely attractive.
