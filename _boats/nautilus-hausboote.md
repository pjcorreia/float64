German houseboat builder, added as the **price-discovery counterweight** to La Mare: narrower hulls (3.6 m beam on the models found), which matters twice — cheaper berths, and *legal road transport* instead of the Apartboat XL's abnormal-load problem.

**Market data (2026-07-29):** used examples on [Yachtall](https://www.yachtall.com/en/boats/nautilus-hausboote): 9.5×3.6 m in Berlin at **€85k**, a 2018 10.9×3.6 m at **€145k**; new range €129k–420k. For context, the broader European used-houseboat market ([Botentekoop](https://www.botentekoop.com/boats/make-houseboat/), [Boat24](https://www.boat24.com/en/powerboats/houseboat/)) runs from €5k shells to €1.9M.
